# OpenRide Community Code of Conduct

OpenRide exists to help people build fairer mobility infrastructure together. Contributors, operators, drivers, riders and community members should be able to participate without harassment or intimidation.

## Our standards

We expect participants to:

- discuss ideas and code on their merits;
- disagree without personal attacks;
- give actionable, respectful review feedback;
- respect privacy, especially identity, location and operational data;
- avoid discrimination, harassment, threats and sexualized conduct;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when they materially affect a proposal;
- avoid manipulating community processes, benchmarks or marketplace discussions;
- remember that transport and safety decisions can affect real people in the physical world.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include harassment, hate speech, doxxing, stalking, threats, repeated unwanted contact, publishing private security details before remediation, deliberate sabotage, impersonation and knowingly submitting malicious code.

## Marketplace discussions

OpenRide is explicitly designed around driver autonomy, rider choice, transparency and self-hosting. Strong disagreement about pricing, ranking, governance and business models is welcome. Attacking people or communities because of those disagreements is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close discussions, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the project and community.

Reports involving sensitive conduct should be sent privately to repository maintainers rather than posted publicly.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, project spaces and other official OpenRide community spaces, as well as when someone is representing OpenRide in public.
